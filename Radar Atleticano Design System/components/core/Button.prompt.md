A pressable action button in the Radar Atleticano tone — gold for the primary call to action, outline and ghost for secondary actions.

```jsx
<Button>Ler matéria</Button>
<Button variant="outline">Ver mais</Button>
<Button variant="ghost" size="sm">Compartilhar</Button>
```

Variants: `primary` (gold fill, black ink), `outline` (hairline border, gold on hover), `ghost` (text only). Sizes `sm | md | lg`. Hover darkens gold / shifts to gold accent; active nudges down 1px. Pass `fullWidth` to stretch.
