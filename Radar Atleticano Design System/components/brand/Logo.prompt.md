The Radar Atleticano logo — a gold vertical bar followed by the uppercase wordmark; use it in headers, footers, and any brand lockup.

```jsx
<Logo />
<Logo variant="mark" size="lg" />
<Logo onLight />
```

Variants: `variant="wordmark"` (default) or `"mark"` (compact RA monogram for favicons/avatars). Sizes `sm | md | lg`. Pass `onLight` when placing on a white surface so the wordmark renders in `--preto`.
